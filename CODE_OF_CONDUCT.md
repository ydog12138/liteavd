# Code of conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, assume good faith, accept
technical correction, and focus criticism on behavior, evidence, and code.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, release channels, and public spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub.
Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. The maintainer
may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or
temporarily/permanently restrict participation in proportion to the behavior.

Good-faith disagreement about architecture, evidence, scope, or risk is welcome
when it remains specific and respectful. Enforcement decisions should protect
participants and the project's ability to collaborate safely.
